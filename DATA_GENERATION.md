# Synthetic data generation

The supplied CSVs are synthetic and were generated with a fixed random seed (42).

The generation process includes:
- seasonal order demand
- customer acquisition channel mix
- product/category price variation
- discounts
- returns/cancellations
- duplicate and missing records
- inconsistent text casing
- marketing-spend seasonality

The dataset is intentionally imperfect so the SQL cleaning stage has a real purpose.
